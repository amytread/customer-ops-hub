Internal Recommendation

Giving customers visibility into product requests

Prepared by Customer Ops · June 2026 · for leadership review

The situation

We already capture every customer request from Intercom into Linear — the integration is live, requests are aggregated, and Linear stays our system of record. What's missing is the customer-facing window: today customers can't see what we've logged, vote on what matters to them, or watch it ship. This recommends how to close that gap.

Recommendation

Go with
Featurebase — a customer-facing roadmap & voting portal, embedded in Tread
Sits on top of Linear (doesn't replace it) · customers log in via Tread (no second password) · ~$59/mo to start

Why Featurebase, all things considered

  • Fits our stack instead of fighting it. Integrates cleanly with both Intercom (which we're keeping) and Linear (our source of truth). The closest competitor, Productlane, is its own support inbox and leans toward replacing Intercom.
  • Pricing scales the right way. We pay per admin seat (the person who triages) — customers are unlimited and free. Canny charges per customer ("tracked users"), so a successful board gets more expensive the more it's used.
  • Everything we want is on one tier. SSO login, voting, and segmented roadmaps all live on the Professional plan — no jump to enterprise.
  • Linear stays the system of record. Votes and requests sync back to the Linear issues we already have. It's a window, not a second database.

The real prize: voting tied to real accounts

Because customers log in as their real Tread account, votes aren't anonymous. We don't just see "47 votes for QuickBooks export" — we see which accounts, weighted by value. That turns the roadmap from guesswork into demand data we can prioritize by ARR. It also cuts duplicate support tickets (customers vote instead of re-asking) and closes the loop automatically when something ships.

Bonus: a roadmap tailored to each customer

🏢 Per-customer roadmaps (optional, same tier)

The same login token can carry each customer's company & tier, so every account can see its own segmented roadmap — their requests plus tier-specific items — alongside the shared overall roadmap. Best used by segment/tier (e.g. Enterprise) rather than a hand-built board per customer. Included on the Professional plan at no extra cost; can be switched on in a later phase.

Honest flags before we commit

  • Confirm the SSO tier with Featurebase sales. Their docs say SSO starts on Professional (~$59/seat); some sources cite a higher "Business" tier. Verify before we quote a number.
  • A voting board needs one owner to triage submissions (merge duplicates, reply, keep it tidy). With no CSMs staffed, that's likely Customer Ops to start — voting lightens the load but doesn't remove it.
  • Recommend launching gated (logged-in customers only) so competitors don't steer our roadmap.

Proposed next step

Confirm Featurebase's SSO pricing, then run a scoped pilot: stand up the overall roadmap, wire the Tread-login handoff, seed it with our existing Linear requests, and open it to a small set of accounts. Validate engagement before a wider rollout — low cost, reversible, no disruption to Intercom or Linear.

Bottom line: It's already all in Linear. Featurebase adds the customer-facing window — voting, visibility, and (optionally) a roadmap per customer — working with our Intercom + Linear as-is, customers free, ~$59/mo for one seat. Confirm the SSO tier, then pilot.
