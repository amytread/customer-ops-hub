How to read this

  • Same-window cohort: % = (issues created and completed inside the window) ÷ (issues created in the window). Numerator and denominator are the same tickets — so backlog purges of older tickets don't inflate it.
  • 1-week reads low by design — tickets opened in the last day or two physically haven't had time to close. The 2-week view is the more meaningful one.
  • "Completed" = status Done. REP work parked in In Review (e.g. REP-317/318/319) is not counted as completed.
  • Open tickets (top strip) = anything not Done, Canceled, or Duplicate — i.e. Triage, Backlog, Todo, In Progress, In Review, etc. — created by the team.
  • In-flight sub-rows show a cumulative rate — completed plus work that's nearly done (REP in In Review, CUS in Pending development; CUS has no "In Progress" status). So "83% w/ in review" = done + in-review as a share of created — a leading indicator of completions about to land.
