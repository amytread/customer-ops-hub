Definition of done

The 10 milestones

Map directly to the columns in the Onboarding Tracker.

  1. 1Settings / Data uploaded
  2. 2Vendors / Drivers onboarded
  3. 3Projects / Orders / Jobs — live dispatchingMost common stall
  4. 4Approve tickets
  5. 5Approve internal driver hours (shifts)
  6. 6Approving driver hours per job
  7. 7Billing — payables, receivables, settlements
  8. 8Foreman approvals
  9. 9Exports / Reports
  10. 10Integration flows

A customer reaches the destination when all 10 are Done. The route's recurring breakdown is milestone 3 — "trained, not yet dispatching." Every stop pushes toward the first live job.

Tailor the path

Enterprise vs. Mid-market

Same route, different emphasis and runway.

Enterprise

  • More coordination on integration flows
  • Longer billing runway — 45–60 days
  • Onsite training common
  • Internal driver hours often N/A

Mid-market

  • Faster path to dispatching
  • More hands-on vendor onboarding
  • Push harder on billing & approvals at Day 21
  • Skip the Day 25 integration stop
